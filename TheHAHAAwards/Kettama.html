KETTAMA

The Irish Heavyweight of Underground House

📖

Biographical Synthesis

Born Evan Campbell in Galway, Ireland, KETTAMA cut his teeth in the local Irish club scene. Drawing inspiration from heavy 90s rave cassettes, Chicago house, and UK bass, he began producing raw, hard-hitting tracks in his bedroom, releasing them independently online and sending them to local DJs.

His breakthrough arrived in 2018 when his tracks began dominating underground club circuits across Europe. Armed with a signature sound of pounding 909 drums and euphoric synth stabs, he quickly ascended from local Galway resident to global festival headliner, launching his own highly successful independent label, G-Town Records, and cementing Ireland's place on the modern techno map.

📀 Seminal Works

In the underground electronic world, EPs and singles dictate success. His Bucklyn EP (2018) on Homage put him on the map, followed by the massive G-Town EP and his collaborative releases on Steel City Dance Discs. His tracks have become staple weapons for DJs globally.

The Underground Anthem

❤️ Philanthropy & Social Impact

Independent Venue Survival

During the COVID-19 pandemic lockdowns, KETTAMA utilized his platform to host massive live-streamed DJ sets, raising funds directly for struggling independent Irish music venues and out-of-work nightlife staff.

G-Town Grassroots Cultivation

Rather than hoarding his success, he uses his independent record label to proactively sign, publish, and financially support young, emerging Irish electronic producers, ensuring the longevity of his hometown's creative ecosystem.

✨ Rave Lore & Trivia

🎥

The Boiler Room Breakout

His legendary 2019 Boiler Room set at the Higher Vision festival in Ireland is widely considered one of the most energetic debuts on the platform, cementing his reputation as a ferocious live performer.

🤝

The Mall Grab Alliance

He forged a massive creative partnership with Australian DJ Mall Grab, frequently performing high-profile back-to-back (B2B) sets globally and releasing tracks on his Steel City Dance Discs imprint.

🏙️

What is G-Town?

"G-Town" is local slang for his hometown of Galway. By naming his label and EPs after the city, he successfully exported local Irish culture to dancefloors across the world.
