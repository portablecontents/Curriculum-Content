LIL UZI VERT

The Rock Star of Emo-Trap • Artist Profile

📖

Biographical Synthesis

Born Symere Bysil Woods on July 31, 1995, in North Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, they grew up listening to Mike Jones, Marilyn Manson, and Paramore. Refusing to settle into the traditional street-rap soundscapes of Philadelphia, Woods began rapping in high school, co-founding a creative collective called Steetown before adopting the moniker Lil Uzi Vert—earned after a childhood friend remarked that their rapid rap flow sounded "like a little Uzi."

Propelled by a vibrant, hyper-energetic, and genre-defying DIY run on SoundCloud, they exploded into global prominence with mixtapes like Luv Is Rage (2015). By combining the aggressive tempos of trap, the emotional vulnerability of 2000s emo-rock, and an avant-garde, gender-fluid fashion sense, Lil Uzi Vert revolutionized contemporary hip-hop, converting underground subcultures into a dominant, chart-conquering global powerhouse.

Lil Uzi Vert commands a remarkably loyal, youth-concentrated, and highly trend-conscious fanbase that bridges mainstream trap listeners with alternative rock and anime subcultures.

Gender Split

Top Global Markets

A true international festival headliner, their largest streaming concentrations lie in North America, Western Europe, and East Asia.

🎵 Medium & Genre

Operating at the boundary of rock and hip-hop, Lil Uzi Vert's medium is characterized by a rapid, high-pitched vocal delivery, rapid triplet flows, and pop-punk vocal belts over heavy 808 sub-bass, synths, and video game chip-tunes.

📀 Seminal Works

Uzi's discography contains era-defining monuments of modern rap. Their groundbreaking studio debut Luv Is Rage 2 (2017) was followed by the highly successful, sci-fi concept album Eternal Atake (2020), and their experimental, genre-bending 2023 powerhouse, Pink Tape.

The Billion-Stream Classic

Their emotional masterpiece "XO Tour Llif3" went organically viral worldwide, amassing over **2.1 Billion** streams on Spotify alone and standing as the defining anthem of the entire SoundCloud rap era.

❤️ Philanthropy & Social Impact

The XXXTentacion Memorial Foundation

Following the tragic loss of close friend and fellow SoundCloud pioneer XXXTentacion, Uzi stepped in to co-found and heavily fund a dedicated memorial foundation aimed at raising millions to support youth mental health, suicide prevention, and single mother assistance.

Philadelphia Youth and Arts Support

Uzi quietly donates massive resources directly to inner-city Philadelphia public school arts programs, community youth shelters, and developmental workshops, providing free musical gear for underprivileged kids.

The $24M Forehead Diamond

In 2021, Uzi captured worldwide media headlines by having a custom, natural 11-carat pink diamond valued at $24 million surgically implanted into their forehead, representing the absolute peak of rockstar excess.

🎸

The Hayley Williams Alliance

A self-professed emo kid, Uzi credits Hayley Williams of Paramore as their single biggest creative inspiration, frequently calling her one of the greatest vocalists and writers of the 21st century.

Anime & Gaming Obsession
