THE ICONS

Welcome to our class-wide digital archive. In this unit, you will step into the role of a cultural critic—analyzing interactive data, evaluating thematic depth, and organizing a cohesive ELA Regents-style argument.

🏆 Your Role: The Selection Committee

You have been appointed to the voting panel of the prestigious **Hauppauge Association of Honored Artists (HAHA) Award**. This career achievement honor recognizes artists who demonstrate exceptional storytelling, monumental quantitative reach, and genuine philanthropic impact on society.

Rather than relying on casual opinions or popularity, you will use our custom-designed **Artist Archive** as your primary database to find, cross-reference, and analyze hard evidence.

🎉 ELA Regents Prep Challenge

To defend your nominations, you must complete the **HAHA Award Argumentative Task**. This task mirrors the critical skills required on Part 2 of the NYS ELA Regents Exam, challenging you to select three artists, back your choices with textual and visual evidence, and systematically dismantle an opposing counterclaim.

📋 Your Source Packet: The 9 Analytical Criteria

As you explore each interactive infographic in the archive, look for evidence across these 9 areas:

📖

1. Biographical Synthesis

Assess how their origins, personal history, and path to fame shaped their artistic perspective.

🎵

2. Artistic Medium & Genre

Analyze how they utilize their primary creative formats to push stylistic boundaries.

🌍

3. Audience Demographics

Examine quantitative data on age distributions, gender splits, and key geographic listener bases.

🎭

4. Thematic Motifs

Evaluate the deeper meaning behind their recurring literary themes, narratives, and social critiques.

💰

5. Financial Valuation

Contrast their commercial success, net worth figures, and business empires with other nominees.

💿

6. Seminal Works & Metrics

Analyze the streaming data and critical reception of their most successful or industry-changing masterpieces.

🏆

7. Critical Acclaim & Awards

Compare peer and institutional praise, including Grammy wins, Oscars, Pulitzers, or Hall of Fame honors.

❤️

8. Philanthropy & Social Impact

Measure their direct positive impact on society through community donations, support campaigns, and activism.

✨

9. Cultural Lore & Trivia

Evaluate unique anecdotes that reveal how their influence extends beyond their primary medium.

🚀 Your Project Steps

How to navigate the archive and build your ELA Regents outline:

01

Browse the Archive

Go to our centralized **Artist Archive** page. Explore the different artist profiles curated for this unit.

02

Analyze the Data

03

04

Defeat the Opponents
