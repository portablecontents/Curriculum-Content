TATE MCRAE

The Choreography-Pop Powerhouse • Artist Profile

📖

Biographical Synthesis

Born July 1, 2003, in Calgary, Canada, Tate Rosner McRae spent her early childhood in Oman before returning to Canada to pursue intense dance training. Performing at an elite level, she became the first Canadian finalist on the American reality show "So You Think You Can Dance" at just 13 years old.

While her athletic dance career was soaring, Tate secretly wrote raw, introspective piano ballads in her bedroom, uploading her first original track "One Day" to YouTube in 2017. The video went viral, triggering a massive bidding war and signing with RCA Records. By fusing her breathtaking, athletic dance abilities with high-tempo, infectious pop hooks, Tate emerged as a rare, complete double-threat visual entertainer on the global pop stage.

❤️ Philanthropy & Social Impact

Supporting Creative Youth & Local Dance Scholarships

Remembering her intense, expensive training years, McRae frequently donates funds, sponsors equipment, and creates competitive scholarships for low-income youth at local Canadian dance academies to help them bypass financial entry barriers.

Mental Health in the Performing Arts

A vocal advocate for young performers, she works with organizations to speak transparently about the intense psychological pressures of early-career success, body image in dance, and prioritizing mental health.

SYTYCD History

At just 13, Tate made history as the very first Canadian finalist on the prominent American show *So You Think You Can Dance*, placing second runner-up in the children's division.

🎹

Bedroom Beginnings

Her viral song "One Day" was written entirely on her bedroom keyboard and filmed on her phone camera, proving her initial organic appeal before any major production backing.

🎙️

Voice Acting Talents

Before her music career dominated her time, Tate was a talented voice actress, lending her voice to characters in animated series such as the Lalaloopsy franchise.
