KALI UCHIS

The Bilingual Queen of Dream-Soul

📖

Biographical Synthesis

Born Karly-Marina Loaiza on July 17, 1994, in Alexandria, Virginia, Kali Uchis spent her childhood split between the United States and her family's native Pereira, Colombia. Finding refuge in music and visual arts during a tumultuous high school experience, she began skipping class to produce her own songs in her bedroom using a cheap microphone, culminating in her 2012 DIY mixtape, Drunken Babble.

Her retro-soul aesthetic quickly caught the attention of major artists like Tyler, the Creator and Snoop Dogg. Refusing to be boxed into a single market, she boldly transitions between English R&B and Spanish-language Latin pop. With universally acclaimed albums like Isolation and Sin Miedo, Uchis has established herself as a formidable, genre-defying architect of modern music.

📀 Seminal Works

Her catalog is a testament to genre versatility. Her debut Isolation (2018) became an instant indie-R&B classic. She followed this by boldly releasing the Spanish-language album Sin Miedo (del Amor y Otros Demonios) (2020), proving her crossover dominance, before returning to lush soul with Red Moon In Venus (2023) and Orquídeas (2024).

The "Telepatía" Phenomenon

The completely Spanish/Spanglish track "telepatía" went organically viral, earning over 1.2 Billion streams and becoming one of the longest-running Spanish-language solos on the Billboard Hot 100.

❤️ Philanthropy & Social Impact

Direct Colombian Community Aid

Uchis frequently returns to Pereira, Colombia, to deliver direct aid. She uses her platform and personal wealth to purchase groceries, medicine, and wheelchairs for struggling families and communities in her native hometown.

Charity Wardrobe Sales & Immigrant Advocacy

She regularly sells pieces from her iconic vintage wardrobe and red-carpet appearances via online thrift platforms, donating 100% of the proceeds to charities supporting immigrant rights and youth community centers.

The Jazz Band Roots

Long before her vocal career took off, she was a dedicated instrumentalist. She played the saxophone and piano in her high school's elite jazz band.

Auteur Director

A true visual artist, Kali conceptualizes and directs almost all of her own lush, highly stylized music videos to maintain absolute control over her aesthetic universe.

The Snooping Car

After recording her first mixtape in her bedroom on a cheap Snowball mic, she used to test her audio mixes by listening to them out of her beat-up Subaru's car speakers.
