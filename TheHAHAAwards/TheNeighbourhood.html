THE NEIGHBOURHOOD

The Architects of California Noir

📖

Biographical Synthesis

Formed in 2011 in Newbury Park, California, The Neighbourhood (often stylized as "THE NBHD") consists of frontman Jesse Rutherford, guitarists Jeremy Freedman and Zach Abels, bassist Mikey Margott, and drummer Brandon Fried. Rejecting the sunny, upbeat pop-rock typical of Southern California at the time, the band cultivated a moody, mysterious, and strictly black-and-white visual identity.

They independently released their breakthrough single, "Sweater Weather," in 2012. The track rapidly transcended genre boundaries, dominating both rock and pop radio globally. By masterfully blending the atmospheric textures of alternative R&B, the heavy, rhythmic drum loops of hip-hop, and the angst of indie rock, The Neighbourhood pioneered a "dark pop" sound that heavily influenced a generation of genre-blurring artists in the 2010s.

🎵 Medium & Genre

The band's sonic medium is defined by Jesse Rutherford's melodic, hip-hop-influenced vocal phrasing, layered over reverb-drenched guitars, deep 808 sub-bass, and driving, mid-tempo drum patterns.

📀 Seminal Works

The band's debut studio album, I Love You. (2013), debuted at number 25 on the Billboard 200 and established their signature moody sound. They followed up with the critically praised, cohesive concept album Wiped Out! (2015) and their synth-heavy, self-titled release The Neighbourhood (2018).

The Diamond Standard

Their breakout hit "Sweater Weather" achieved the historic, highly coveted **RIAA Diamond Certification** (over 10 million units sold in the US), cementing it as one of the defining alternative anthems of the decade.

✨ California Noir Lore & Trivia

🖤

The B&W Mandate

For the first several years of their career, the band adhered to a strict, uncompromising black-and-white visual aesthetic across all music videos, photography, artwork, and stage designs to emphasize their moody themes.

🇬🇧

The British Spelling

Despite being from California, the band chose to use the British spelling of "Neighbourhood" on the advice of their manager, simply because the American spelling was already taken by a different band.

🎙️

Hip-Hop Production Roots

Frontman Jesse Rutherford was heavily influenced by hip-hop production and mixtape culture, which is why the band frequently incorporates 808 drum machines, beat-switches, and rap-style cadences into their rock arrangements.
