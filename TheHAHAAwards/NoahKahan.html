NOAH KAHAN

The Troubadour of New England • Artist Profile

🌲

Biographical Synthesis

Born January 1, 1997, in Strafford, Vermont, Noah Kahan spent his childhood split between small forest towns in Vermont and New Hampshire. Writing songs from the age of eight, his raw, therapeutic indie-folk-pop style caught the ear of local producers, landing him a contract with Republic Records in 2017.

Despite early success with radio-pop singles, the pandemic-era isolation of 2020 pulled Noah back to his rural Vermont family home. In that cabin-fever climate, he abandoned traditional pop production to write raw, acoustic songs about local roads, seasonal depression, and small-town nostalgia. The resulting 2022 album, Stick Season, became a global cultural phenomenon, earning him the nickname "Vermont's Favorite Son" and turning his regional New England diaries into universal anthems.

🎵 Medium & Genre

Noah Kahan’s vocal delivery blends conversational folk-singing with soaring, emotional high-notes, backed primarily by fingerpicked acoustic guitars, driving mandolins, and banjos.

📀 Seminal Works

Noah's earlier albums, Busyhead (2019) and I Was / I Am (2021), highlighted his indie pop potential. However, his self-written, 2022 masterpiece Stick Season and its expanded deluxe edition, Stick Season (We'll All Be Here Forever), cemented him as a generation-defining folk singer.

❤️ Philanthropy & Social Impact

The Busyhead Project (Founder)

A passionate, highly active advocate for mental health. Noah founded **The Busyhead Project**, raising over **$2.5 Million** to support democratic access to therapy, funding local and national mental health organizations, particularly in rural, low-income, and isolated communities.

Ticket Sales Philanthropy

Allocates $1 from every ticket sold on his global headlining tours directly to The Busyhead Project, using his massive touring engine to fund critical mental health services globally.

✨ Cultural Lore & Trivia

🍂

The True "Stick Season"

The phrase "Stick Season" is a Vermont-specific slang term for the desolate, gray period between autumn leaf-peeping and the first winter snowfall, which Noah used as a metaphor for transitional depression.

🤝

Collaborative Magnet

Following the album's success, music titans rushed to record duets with him. He released official collabs on *Stick Season* tracks with Olivia Rodrigo, Post Malone, Kacey Musgraves, Hozier, and Brandi Carlile.

🏫

High School Writing

Before performing in sold-out arenas, Noah wrote songs in high school, playing local open-mic nights across Vermont and New Hampshire to master his performance craft.
