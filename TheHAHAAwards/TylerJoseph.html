TYLER JOSEPH

Frontman of Twenty One Pilots • Artist Profile

📖

Biographical Synthesis

Born December 1, 1988, in Columbus, Ohio, Tyler Joseph grew up in a devout, athletic household. A standout high school basketball player, he was offered a full collegiate athletic scholarship. However, after discovering an old, dusty electronic keyboard in his closet, he rejected the sports path to teach himself music, copying radio tunes and writing raw poetry.

In 2009, he formed **Twenty One Pilots** with high school friends, later solidifying the iconic, energetic duo in 2011 with drummer **Josh Dun**. Blending synth-pop hooks, rapid-fire rap, and highly theatrical conceptual storylines, Tyler’s intensely vulnerable songwriting connected directly with millions, taking them from basement shows in Ohio to packing the world's largest arenas.

🎵 Medium & Genre

Tyler operates as a multi-instrumentalist, utilizing keyboards, bass, synthesizers, and his signature ukulele. His genre-blending style shifts dynamically from upbeat synth-pop and reggae grooves to aggressive alternative hip-hop verses.

    Insecurity & "Blurryface" Personification

📀 Seminal Works

Tyler's catalog contains historic, chart-altering conceptual projects. Their third record, Vessel (2013), built their cult fame. However, the landmark release Blurryface (2015) shattered music history, becoming the first album ever to have *every single song* certified Gold or Platinum by the RIAA, followed by the highly conceptual Trench (2018) and Clancy (2024).

The Diamond Standard

"Stressed Out" stands as an era-defining, RIAA-certified Diamond global hit single, generating over 2.2 Billion streams on Spotify alone.

❤️ Philanthropy & Social Impact

National Suicide Prevention & Crisis Partners

Given the mental health focus of his catalog, Tyler is a massive advocate for suicide prevention. He partners with and donates substantial proceeds from tours directly to the American Foundation for Suicide Prevention (AFSP) and the Trevor Project to provide critical youth crisis resources.

Local Columbus Youth & Arts Endowments

Quietly funds and sponsors youth arts programs, music therapy initiatives, and Columbus local public school music departments to provide free instruments and safe creative outlets for kids in his home city.

✨ Clique Lore & Trivia

✈️

Arthur Miller Origins

Tyler named the band after Arthur Miller's classic play *All My Sons*, where a father knowingly ships faulty aircraft parts causing the death of 21 pilots, symbolizing moral dilemmas and tough choices.

🩳

Pants-less Grammy Move

Before they were famous, Tyler and Josh used to watch the Grammys in their underwear in their shared apartment. True to a pact they made, they accepted their 2017 Grammy in their underwear live on stage.

📍

On-Stage "X" Tattoos

To cement their eternal bond with their local Columbus hometown fanbase, both Tyler and Josh received matching "X" tattoos live on stage during a hometown show in 2013.
