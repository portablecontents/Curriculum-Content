The Origin Story

Before the record-breaking streams and sold-out stadium tours, Aubrey "Drake" Graham was born on October 24, 1986, in Toronto, Canada. His initial taste of fame didn't come from music, but as an actor on the teen drama series Degrassi: The Next Generation in the early 2000s.

Driven by an unrelenting passion for music, he released his first mixtape in 2006. His unique blend of singing and rapping caught the industry's attention, leading him to sign with Young Money Entertainment in 2009. This pivotal moment launched him into a full-time music career that would reshape the modern pop and hip-hop landscape.

Drake's monumental success is fueled by a massive, diverse global audience. Analyzing his listenership provides insight into his universal appeal across different ages, genders, and continents.

Age Distribution

The 18-34 demographic constitutes the overwhelming majority (65%) of his listener base.

Gender Split

His music maintains an incredibly balanced appeal between male and female listeners.

Top Geographic Markets

The Anglosphere dominates his top markets, led heavily by the United States and his native Canada.

🏆 Most Popular Work

Drake's discography is a collection of commercial juggernauts. Albums like Take Care (2011), Views (2016), and Scorpion (2018) defined their respective eras.

Historic Milestone

He became the first artist in music history to surpass 500 Million RIAA-certified units across singles and albums.

Spotify Billion-Stream Club

✨ The Drake Lore: Interesting Facts

📈

The "Drake Effect"

He is famous in the industry for instantly boosting lesser-known artists' careers simply by appearing as a guest feature on their songs.

✈️

Air Drake

An aviation enthusiast, he owns a completely customized, 767 wide-body cargo plane named "Air Drake," valued at an estimated $185 million.

🚫

Grammy Boycott

Despite multiple wins early on, Drake has historically skipped attending or submitting his music to the ceremony, publicly criticizing the voting process.
