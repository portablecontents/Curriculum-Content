CRITIC'S BLUEPRINT

STUDENT PROJECT CHECKLIST

Follow this step-by-step visual roadmap to explore our class's interactive Artist Archive, analyze primary source metrics, and construct your ELA Regents-style argument outline.

Phase 1: Close Reading & Database Exploration
1
Step 1

Access the Archive

Navigate to the **"Artist Archive"** directory page on our class Google Site. This custom-curated digital directory acts as your official ELA Regents "Source Packet."

2
Step 2

Explore at Least 5 Artists

Open at least **five different artist profiles** on our site. Interact with their visual elements: hover over demographic charts, compare net worth metrics, and closely read their **Thematic Motifs**, **Major Awards**, and **Philanthropy** panels.

3
Step 3

Choose Your Nominees

Select the **three artists** who you believe most deserve to be awarded the HAHA (Hauppauge Association of Honored Artists) Award based on their cumulative artistic achievements, demographic reach, and philanthropic contributions.

Phase 2: Evidence Gathering & Synthesis
4
Step 4

Extract Hard Evidence

Open your **HAHA Award: Argument Outline Organizer**. Search the infographics to pull both **quantitative** (numeric) and **qualitative** (thematic/descriptive) evidence to back your choices.

📈 **Quantitative:** Sourcing specific data percentages (e.g., Calvin Harris's 75% Gen-Z listenership) or stream counts.

🌱 **Qualitative:** Sourcing specific social actions (e.g., Billie Eilish's REVERB clean-energy tour partnerships).

5
Step 5

Cite Your Sourced Data

For every piece of evidence you use, you must write a formal citation crediting the curator of that page to establish source credibility.

Regents Citation Style:
"According to the profile on Kendrick Lamar curated by [Classmate Name]..."
Phase 3: Writing the ELA Regents-Style Argument
6
Step 6

Complete the Outline Organizer

Build your argument from the ground up:

  • Outline your two primary supporting body paragraphs.
  • Establish a clear **counterclaim** recognizing a massive, highly successful artist you *did not* include in your top three.
  • Construct a strong **rebuttal** proving why your chosen trio remains more deserving of the award despite the counterclaim's achievements.
7
Step 7

Craft Your Thesis Statement

After completing all body paragraph points, analyze your reasoning. Synthesize your entire argument into a single, elegant, and powerful thesis statement at the very end of your organizer to complete the writing process.

✍️ Synthesize claim, reasons, and rebuttal perspectives

🎓 Ready for Phase 2 Evaluation

Use this visual checklist as you explore each published page. Reach out to your classmates to clarify their thematic points and check all metrics before finalizing your draft!
