DANIEL CAESAR

The Modern Voice of Neo-Soul • Artist Profile

📖

Biographical Synthesis

Born Ashton Dumar Norwill Simmonds on July 30, 1995, in Oshawa, Ontario, Caesar was raised in a strict, deeply religious Seventh-day Adventist household. After clashing with his parents over his musical ambitions, he was kicked out of his home at age 17, moving to Toronto where he slept in parks and busked on streets while recording early acoustic tracks.

His breakthrough arrived independently in 2017 with his self-funded, Grammy-nominated debut album Freudian. Fusing raw acoustic soul, gospel choirs, and vulnerable contemporary R&B harmonies, Caesar established himself as a dominant creative force, bypassing traditional major label avenues to achieve global critical acclaim.

❤️ Philanthropy & Social Impact

Canadian Creative Youth Collectives

Remembering his struggles as a homeless teenager in Toronto, Caesar actively funds and donates recording gear to independent creative spaces and youth shelters in Ontario, giving low-income artists access to professional studios.

Mental Health & Marginalized Community Support

Publicly advocates for therapy, speaking transparently about his struggles with sudden isolation and faith crises, and routinely donates performance earnings to organizations offering mental health counseling in inner-city communities.
