MALCOLM TODD

The Groovy Prince of Bedroom R&B • Artist Profile

📖

Biographical Synthesis

Born and raised in Los Angeles, California, Malcolm Todd represents the vanguard of a new, highly creative wave of internet-native bedroom R&B musicians. Picking up the bass and guitar as a teenager, he began recording raw, groove-oriented alternative pop tracks in his bedroom, clashing classical jazz chord progressions with catchy, conversational vocals.

His breakthrough occurred in 2023 when EPs like Art House and his viral smash "Roommates" exploded on TikTok and Spotify. By rejecting hyper-polished pop formulas and writing tongue-in-cheek songs about awkward youthful romances over thunderous, self-played funky basslines, Todd quickly built a massive, dedicated following, establishing himself as a key architect of modern Gen Z indie-soul.

Todd's musical medium is heavily acoustic and organic yet modern. Known for his thick, driving funk basslines and crisp guitar chords, he seamlessly blends alternative pop writing with hazy, bedroom R&B tempos.
