PART 1: The Engine (90/10 Split)

🟥 The MAJOR Bucket (90%)

These are the non-negotiable anchors of the quarter. They are the heavy-lifters: Formal Essays, Midterm Portfolios, and Unit Mastery Tests.

🟨 The MINOR Bucket (10%)

This is the Daily Engine. This bucket is filled by your daily grind: Dial-ins, annotations, AI revision checks, and independent reading.

Graded via additive XP/points.
