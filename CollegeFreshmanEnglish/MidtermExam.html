Midterm Exam Protocol // CFE ENG101

Midterm Directive

Engage in self-reflection and critical thinking about your growth as a writer. By selecting, revising, and presenting your best work, you will demonstrate your ability to analyze your writing and revise it thoughtfully based on feedback. The final portfolio should showcase your writing skills and growth over the first two quarters.

Part 1: The Artifacts

Select three original pieces of writing from Quarter 1 and Quarter 2 to serve as the foundation of your portfolio. Reflect different types of writing (essays, reader responses, creative writing, journals, etc.).

Required Elements for EACH Piece:

  • General Info Table: Title, Type of assignment, & Unit of Study.
  • Original Version: The text exactly as initially submitted.
  • Revised Version: A more evolved version based on feedback.*
  • Revision Reflection: Detail the steps taken. What changed and why? How did feedback inform changes? What was challenging?

*Note: If including your college essay, it requires no additional revision, just the reflection.

Part 2: Midterm Reflection

Write a thoughtful, well-organized 1–2 page reflection displaying your skills as a writer and editor. Ensure you structure your ideas into paragraphs and address the following 6 prompts:

1. Selection Process

Discuss the process of choosing each piece for the portfolio.

2. Point of Pride

Which writing piece are you most proud of and why?

3. The Power of Revision

How did the revision process help improve your writing?

4. Writer's Confidence

How has your confidence in writing changed since the start of the semester?

5. Strengths & Weaknesses

What have you learned about your strengths and weaknesses as a writer?

6. Strategies for Growth

What areas of writing do you still feel you need to improve in, and what strategies will you use to continue growing as a writer?

Evaluation Criteria

By curating your work and reflecting on your progress, you can take pride in your achievements and see how far you've come. Ensure you work the processes of brainstorming, rough drafting, editing, and revision.
