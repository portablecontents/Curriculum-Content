Final Exam Protocol // CFE ENG101

Final Directive

Engage in self-reflection and critical thinking about your growth as a writer. By selecting, revising, and presenting your best work, you will demonstrate your ability to analyze your writing and revise it thoughtfully based on feedback. The final portfolio should showcase your writing skills and growth over your entire academic career.

Part 1: The Artifacts

Select two original pieces of writing to serve as the foundation of your portfolio. They can be essays, reader responses, creative writing, reflections, or journal entries.

Selection #1

Must be from Quarter 3 or Quarter 4 of this academic year.

Selection #2

May be from any English class K-12 (Cannot be a piece previously used on your midterm).

Required Elements for EACH Piece:

  • General Info Table: Title, Type of assignment, & Unit of Study.
  • Original Version: The text exactly as initially submitted.
  • Revised Version: A more evolved version based on feedback.*
  • Revision Reflection: Detail the steps taken. What changed and why? How did feedback inform changes? What was challenging?

*Note: If including your college essay, it requires no additional revision, just the reflection.

Part 2: Final Reflection

Write a thoughtful, well-organized 1–2 page reflection tracing your growth, naming your strengths, and considering how your learning will carry forward. Address the following 6 prompts:

1. Portfolio Choices & Growth

What do your selections reveal about the writing you value or your voice? How do they show growth?

2. Defining Milestones

Which assignment (K-12) was most important to your development? What did it teach you?

3. Revision as Transformation

How has your understanding of revision changed? Give specific examples of reworking a piece.

4. Confidence & Identity

Describe your confidence now vs. entering high school. What shifted your mindset?

5. Reading, Writing, & Thinking

How has your approach evolved? What role does writing play in how you make sense of the world?

6. Lifelong Learning

What habits will you carry beyond high school? Where do you still see room for growth?

Evaluation Criteria

The reflection should display your skills as a writer and editor. Ensure you work the processes of brainstorming, rough drafting, editing, and revision.
